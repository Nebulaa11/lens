Connect the feed.

01

Create your Analytics Token

Sign in to Upstox Developer Apps → Analytics → Generate Token. Keep the token private.

02

Configure the local backend

Add the token to the server environment as UPSTOX_ACCESS_TOKEN. It stays on your computer and is never sent to this page.

03

Start the live connection

Run the included server.py, then select Live data. Cards update from received quotes, with provider timestamps. Missing analytics remain unavailable.

Read the market,
at a glance.

Follow sectors on the left through the conceptual connecting flow to up to 40 compact stock profiles and six ranked outputs. The top three on each side are highlighted. Preview mode uses fixtures. Live mode only displays received provider data.

01

See the bigger picture

The dots show stock movements. The session lines compare each stock with its opening price. Click a sector to narrow the view.

02

Find a stronger setup

Green cards show upward sample trends; pink cards show downward trends. Click a stock to inspect its candles, zones and levels.

03

Explore the shortlist

Search with /, filter a sector or direction, and save stocks with the star. Open a card to inspect its details; Escape closes the popup.
